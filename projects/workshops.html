What was done

Tracing From Screen to Page was a GLINA³ workshop at NADD Archive Day: Crafting the Archive at Nieuwe Instituut. Participants traced symbols from early digital culture by hand and shaped the drawings into clay tiles for a collective mural, connecting screen-based images with touch, digital memory and archival gaps.
