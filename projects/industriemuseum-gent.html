Textile research on the TC2 jacquard loom

Industriemuseum Gent

A museum residency moving from archival letters and digital composition to dyed yarn and two woven tapestries.

  • Year27 November–14 December 2025
  • RoleDesigner and researcher in residence
  • ForIndustriemuseum Gent — Makers aan de TC2

What was done

The residency produced two tapestries on the museum’s digitally programmable TC2 jacquard loom. My role covered archive-led pattern development, digital composition, jacquard-file preparation and weaving; together with Emile Lupatini, the yarns were dyed in the museum’s dye workshop.

The process moved from digital composition to technically prepared loom files, material and colour tests, and independent production inside a specialist museum environment.

Delivered

  • Archive-led pattern research;
  • Digital composition and TC2 file preparation;
  • Jacquard loom setup and weaving;
  • Material and colour tests;
  • Yarn dyeing with Emile Lupatini;
  • Two finished tapestries;
