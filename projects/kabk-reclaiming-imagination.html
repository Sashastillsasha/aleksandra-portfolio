Workshop design · AI · education

Reclaiming Imagination

Restoring Missing Knowledge Through Material and Digital Practice

  • Year2026
  • RoleWorkshop co-designer and facilitator
  • ForMaster Artistic Research, KABK

What was done

With Timo Bega, I co-developed and facilitated a workshop for KABK’s Master of Arts in Fine Art and Design project week. We built a learning pipeline that moved from research and mapping to software experiments, material tests and speculative objects.

Students explored how tools such as TouchDesigner, 3D scanning and Blender could be learned and applied in the context of AI. Rather than treating AI as a shortcut or a finished answer, the workshop used it openly as part of a critical research and making process. Participants worked with small archives and missing knowledge to develop material responses and imagine alternative futures.

Workshop structure

  • Research and collect source material;
  • Map gaps, absences and questions;
  • Learn and test digital tools;
  • Connect software processes with material practice;
  • Develop speculative outcomes through iteration.
