Editorial research with Critical Playground

Digital Ornament

An editorial project about surface, technology and visual culture, developed with clear image rights and reusable collaboration terms.

  • Year2024
  • RoleResearcher, editor and image-rights coordinator
  • ForCritical Playground

What was done

My role combined editorial research and rights coordination: researching and verifying archival and contemporary images, contacting contributors and rightsholders, and coordinating captions and credits.

The Free Art License 1.3 established clear terms for attribution, reuse and transformation in collaborations with artists, supporting the project as both an editorial publication and a community resource.

Delivered

  • Editorial and visual research;
  • Contributor and rightsholder contact;
  • Image verification, captions and credits;
  • Free Art License collaboration terms;
  • Publication and community communication;
