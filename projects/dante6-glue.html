Three-day brand activation

DANTE6 × GLUE Amsterdam

A three-day fashion and design activation connecting the brand, the physical space, invited creatives and a live public programme.

  • Year17–19 September 2026
  • RoleCreative producer and event coordinator
  • ForDANTE6 during GLUE Amsterdam

What was done

My role covered the activation from concept through scheduling, suppliers, contributors and on-site production. The programme combined a window installation, custom lighting, printed graphics, a DJ, performers and invited creators.

The same visual language continued through invitations, social content, photography direction and the in-store experience, while I managed a project budget of approximately €20K.

Delivered

  • Event concept and three-day programme;
  • Budget, schedule and supplier coordination;
  • Window graphics and printed materials;
  • Lighting and spatial presentation;
  • Creator, performer and DJ coordination;
  • Invitations, social content and photography direction;
  • On-site delivery;
