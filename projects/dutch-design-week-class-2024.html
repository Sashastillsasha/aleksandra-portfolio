Dutch Design Week · Klokgebouw, Eindhoven

Class of 2024

A public presentation of Weaving Narratives of Hope during Dutch Design Week, bringing the film, publication and woven research into one installation.

  • Year19–27 October 2024
  • RoleArtist, researcher and exhibition designer
  • ForClass of 2024 at Dutch Design Week, Eindhoven

What was done

Weaving Narratives of Hope was translated into an exhibition format for the Class of 2024 presentation at Klokgebouw during Dutch Design Week. The installation brought together the documentary, publication and woven material so visitors could enter the project through several forms.

My role covered the spatial presentation, material selection and public hosting during the programme.

Delivered

  • Exhibition concept and spatial presentation;
  • Film, publication and textile selection;
  • Installation and on-site production;
  • Public hosting and project communication;
