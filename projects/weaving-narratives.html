Research, documentary film & publication

Weaving Narratives of Hope

MA research bringing women’s industrial knowledge into view through interviews, a documentary film, a publication and material experiments.

  • Year2023–2024
  • RoleResearcher, director, editor and publication designer
  • ForMA thesis, KABK / Leiden University

What was done

The project grew from research into the decline of a regional hemp industry and the knowledge held by former factory workers. My particular role included 12 qualitative interviews, coordination of an interdisciplinary film team, direction and the full edit of the 23-minute documentary.

The research then became a publication and a series of material experiments, allowing the project to move across academic, public and visual formats without losing the voices at its centre.

Delivered

  • Qualitative field research;
  • Interview planning and 12 interviews;
  • Film direction and team coordination;
  • Full edit of a 23-minute documentary;
  • Archival research and rights coordination;
  • Publication design and material experiments;
  • Public and festival presentation;
