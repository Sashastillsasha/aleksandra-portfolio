Research, documentary film & publication

Weaving Narratives of Hope

MA research bringing women’s industrial knowledge into view through interviews, a documentary film, a publication and material experiments.

  • Year2023–2024
  • RoleResearcher, director, editor and publication designer
  • ForMA Industrial Design, Royal Academy of Art, The Hague (KABK)

What was done

Weaving Narratives of Hope: In the Ruins of the Hemp Industry is my 2024 Master’s graduation research in Industrial Design at the Royal Academy of Art, The Hague (KABK). It began with six days of ethnographic fieldwork in Mordovia and the Penza region, investigating the decline of the regional hemp industry and the knowledge held by former workers and local communities.

I conducted 12 qualitative interviews and archival research, then translated the findings into a 23-minute documentary road film, a publication and material experiments. A TC2-woven textile brings local hemp and reclaimed wool into conversation with digital pattern making. The project is documented by Redesigning the Industry and the KABK Graduation Catalogue, and appears in e-flux’s Graduation Show announcement.
