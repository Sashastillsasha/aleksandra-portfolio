Design Biennale Rotterdam

Gtree

A selected presentation exploring how nature can be interpreted through code, a pen plotter and digitally generated graphic processes.

  • Year20 February–2 March 2025
  • RoleConcept, visual research and presentation
  • ForDesign Biennale Rotterdam at Katoenhuis

What was done

Developed with Timo Bega, Gtree examined the relationship between materiality, technology and production. Coded interpretations of natural forms moved through pen-plotter drawings and digitally generated patterns into an installation.

My role centred on concept, visual research and public presentation, making the process legible from computational source to physical result.

Delivered

  • Concept and visual research;
  • Code-led graphic experimentation;
  • Pen-plotter process;
  • Exhibition selection and installation;
  • Public presentation at Katoenhuis;
